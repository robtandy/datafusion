# Morsel vs. Stock Gap Diagnosis: TPC-H SF5 Q4, Q11, Q16, and Q22 (#216)

## Executive Summary

In the Wave-4 22/22 TPC-H SF5 scorecard (`#209`,
[sf5_22q_wave4_scorecard.md](../morsel_tpch/sf5_22q_wave4_scorecard.md)),
single-config `morsel_pushdown` (`morsel_mode=required`) beats both
`stock_default` (`0.83×` geomean) and `stock_pushdown` (`0.90×`), but still
trails `stock_default` on four small-to-medium queries (`125–230 ms` total wall
time):

| Query           | `stock_default` | `stock_pushdown` | `morsel_no_pd` | `morsel_pushdown` | `duckdb_default` | Gap vs. `stock_default` | Primary Root Cause(s)                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------- | --------------: | ---------------: | -------------: | ----------------: | ---------------: | ----------------------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **`q16`**       |    **132.6 ms** |         143.4 ms |       169.5 ms |          172.0 ms |         141.0 ms |    **+39.4 ms (1.30×)** | `stage_0` cap-4 channel throttling 8 radix finalizers (`~20–26 ms`) + sequential 1-RG `supplier` build (`14.6 ms`) + forced `RowFilter` on 8-RG `part` (`~8–10 ms`)                  |
| **`q4`**        |    **201.1 ms** |         291.4 ms |       311.3 ms |          226.5 ms |         191.7 ms |    **+25.4 ms (1.13×)** | `JoinBloomFilterExpr` (`26.4%` cycles / `377 ms` CPU on 30M rows) + unspecialized `BuildSemi` loop (`4.8%` in `pd`, `64.7%` in `no_pd`) + `keep_dynamic_preds_in_bulk=false`         |
| **`q11`**       |    **112.9 ms** |         127.9 ms |       190.3 ms |          135.2 ms |          95.7 ms |    **+22.3 ms (1.20×)** | 25–32/64 `partsupp` RGs trapped in slow `RowFilter` during adaptive probe + Bloom dropped in `BulkDecode` + 4 serial 1-RG builds (`7.5 ms`) + `SingleHashTable` vs. Stock `ArrayMap` |
| **`q22`**       |    **124.0 ms** |         142.3 ms |       169.0 ms |          126.3 ms |         144.7 ms |     **+2.3 ms (1.02×)** | Same unspecialized `BuildAnti` (`18.2%` cycles) + scalar Bloom (`12.7%` cycles) + 6-RG `customer` core starvation (`119.1 ms` `ready_wait`)                                          |
| **4-Query Sum** |    **570.6 ms** |         705.0 ms |       840.1 ms |      **660.0 ms** |         573.1 ms |    **+89.4 ms (1.16×)** | **Closable to ~536–568 ms with Ranks 1–5 (includes unmeasured `q4` fused-Bloom estimate; `~580 ms` from ablation-proven toggles alone)**                                             |

Every finding below is backed by: (1) quiet-host SF=5 Wave-4 baseline runs on
`rob-tandy-dduck2` (`78e46e910`, `92–93%` host idle, 100% warm `mincore` page
residency, 8 pinned ARM Neoverse-N1 cores), (2) quiet-host side-by-side physical
plans and Stock `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` (`profile_4q.py plans`, `93%` host idle), (3)
Arm PMUv3 hardware counters (`perf stat`: `cycles:u`, `instructions:u`, IPC,
`r17:u` L2d cache refills), (4) `perf record` leaf CPU profiles (`3,999 Hz
cycles:u`, `0` lost samples, 22K–62K samples per profile), and (5) quiet-host
reproducible scratch phase-timeline probes and 5-round counterbalanced ablations
(`q4_q11_q16_q22/ablations.patch` + `profile_4q.py ablations`, `93%` idle pre /
`98–99%` idle post, 100% Arrow table parity verified).

**Key headline from quiet-host scratch ablations
(`q4_q11_q16_q22/ablations.patch`):**

- **On `q16`:** Widening `ensure_stage`'s output channel from `4 → 16`
  (`result_capacity(8)`, within `MAX_RESULT_CAPACITY = 32`) or `64`
  (`dag.rs:660`) **alone** cuts `stage_0` `agg_emit` wall time **`2.1×`
  (`32.7 → 15.6 ms`)** and total exec by **`−22.8 to −26.1 ms`**. Combining
  `stage_cap`, concurrent independent builds (`part` + `supplier`: `−11.7 to
−15.2 ms`), avoiding forced `RowFilter` on `part` (`8 < 12` RGs: `−10.2 ms`),
  and aligned NEON Bloom cuts quiet `q16` exec by **`−39.0 to −44.4 ms`**
  (`164.9 → 120.5 ms`, `par: 4.40× → 5.84×`), closing the `+39.4 ms` Stock gap
  and projecting **`~128–134 ms`** total (matching/edging `stock_default`
  `132.6 ms` and beating DuckDB `141.0 ms`).
- **On `q22`:** Adding a batch-inlined flat-int `BuildSemi`/`BuildAnti` probe
  loop (`probe_mark_radix_flat_int`) cuts quiet `q22` probe process time
  **`2.29×` (`112.0 → 49.0 ms`)**, exec CPU by **`−62.8 to −65.9 ms`**, and
  exec wall by **`−8.4 to −8.7 ms`**; adding aligned NEON Bloom yields
  **`−67.0 to −70.0 ms` CPU** (`−8.3 to −9.9 ms` wall), projecting `q22` to
  **`~114–119 ms`** (`−5 to −10 ms` faster than `stock_default`, `−26 to
−31 ms` faster than DuckDB).
- **On `q4`:** The `+110 ms` `morsel_no_pushdown` deficit is `64.7%`
  unspecialized `BuildSemi` probing across 64 radix partition tables (`8.0 MiB`
  random scatter, IPC `1.46`, `23.99M` L2d refills); `probe_mark_radix_flat_int`
  alone cuts `no_pushdown` by **`−480.8 ms` CPU (`−59.9 ms` wall)**, and
  `bloom_fast + fast_semi` / `force_bulk + keep_dyn + bloom_fast + fast_semi`
  cut quiet `morsel_pushdown` by **`−12.1 to −15.4 ms` wall** (`−28 to
−70 ms` CPU). Closing the remaining `~10–13 ms` gap is analytically estimated
  (unmeasured) to require fusing a compact L2-resident 1-word (64-bit)
  register-blocked Bloom prefilter directly into `probe_mark_radix_flat_int`
  (operating on the `18.97M` post-`l_receiptdate > l_commitdate` rows after
  `407.5 ms` bulk decode instead of `377 ms` `RowFilter` Bloom on all `30M`
  rows), projecting **`~184–198 ms`** (`−28 to −42 ms` vs. current `226.5 ms`).
- **On `q11`:** Quiet `[DIAG_ADAPT]` probes show `partsupp` runs
  `1.02×–1.21×` faster in `BulkDecode` than `RowFilter` (`92.7–96.4` vs.
  `104.5–112.3 ns/row`), yet wave-1/2 probing leaves `25–32 / 64` RGs in slow
  `RowFilter`, and `keep_dynamic_preds_in_bulk = false` drops the 4 KiB
  L1-resident Bloom filter once switched. Keeping dynamic filters in `BulkDecode`
  and eliminating the wave-1 `RowFilter` tax saves **`−12.9 ms` wall (`−72.5 ms`
  CPU)** in quiet ablation (`force_bulk + keep_dyn + bloom_fast`), while adding
  a dense `ArrayMap`/bitset join path closes the remaining **`−6 to −10 ms`**
  (`135.2 → ~110–116 ms`). Optional Stage CSE (reusing `main`'s grouped sum for
  the `55.7 ms` `scalar_sq_0` stage) would cut `q11` by **`−48 to −55 ms`** to
  **`~68–76 ms`**, beating DuckDB (`95.7 ms`).

### 3.2 TPC-H Q4: `226.5 ms` vs. `stock_default 201.1 ms` (`+25.4 ms` / `1.13×`; `morsel_no_pushdown 311.3 ms`)

#### Physical Plan, Join Direction, and `o_orderdate` Pruning

- **Identical join direction and zero RG pruning:** Both Stock and Morsel place
  filtered `orders` (`o_orderdate >= '1993-07-01' AND < '1993-10-01'`, `287,250`
  rows = `3.83%`) on the **build** side (`LeftSemi`, `build=Left`) and probe
  with `lineitem` (`l_receiptdate > l_commitdate`, `18.97M` rows = `63.23%`).
  Inspecting SF=5 Parquet row-group statistics confirms **every single one of
  the 64 `orders` RGs** spans `min(o_orderdate) = 1992-01-01` to
  `max(o_orderdate) = 1998-08-02` (because `orders` is sorted by `o_orderkey`,
  not `o_orderdate`), and the `287,250` matching keys span `o_orderkey in
[193..29,999,589]`, overlapping all `240 / 240` `lineitem` RGs. All four arms
  scan the exact same `166.97 MiB` (`orders 33.5 MiB + lineitem 133.5 MiB`).
- **Why `stock_default` (`201.1 ms`) beats `stock_pushdown` (`291.4 ms`) by
  `90.3 ms`:** Quiet `profile_4q.py plans` `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` shows
  `stock_pushdown` pushes `l_receiptdate > l_commitdate AND
DynamicFilter(hash_lookup)` into Parquet `RowFilter`, spending **`832.0 ms` in
  `row_pushdown_eval_time`** (`lineitem DataSourceExec = 1.33 s` vs. `397.5 ms`
  in `stock_default`). By contrast, `stock_default` bulk-decodes `orders`
  (`255.5 ms`) and `lineitem` (`397.5 ms`, total `653.0 ms`), filters
  `l_receiptdate > l_commitdate` (`60.3 ms`), hash-repartitions `lineitem`
  (`repartition_time = 137.7 ms`), and probes 8 **L2-resident** partitioned hash
  tables (`35.9K` rows ≈ `570 KiB` per core; `HashJoinExec = 174.1 ms`, total
  `exec CPU = 1,278 ms`, IPC `1.90`).

#### Why `morsel_no_pushdown` Is `+110.2 ms` Slower (`311.3 ms`, `2,215 ms` CPU)

Morsel's bulk Parquet scans (`orders 265.5 ms + lineitem 407.5 ms = 673.0 ms`
CPU) match `stock_default`'s quiet `DataSourceExec` (`255.5 + 397.5 = 653.0 ms`
CPU). The entire `+110 ms` deficit (`+937 ms` CPU!) comes from ONE path:
`HashJoinProbe::next_chunk_radix` (`hash_join.rs:4519–4544`) for
`BuildSemi`/`BuildAnti`:

1. **No specialized batch loop (`64.7%` of all cycles!):** Unlike
   `Inner`/`BuildOuter` (which use `probe_collect_radix_flat_int`), `BuildSemi`
   loops row-by-row calling `probe_row_at` (`5.88%`) → `ProbeKeys::hash_at`
   (`2.42%`, 1st `mix_u64` + enum dispatch) → `probe_mark_all_matches`
   (`46.15%`, 2nd enum dispatch + **2nd `mix_u64`**!) + `next_chunk_radix`
   overhead (`10.29%`).
2. **L2 cache thrashing across 64 radix tables (`8.0 MiB` random scatter):**
   Because `287,250 > RADIX_JOIN_THRESHOLD_ROWS (16,384)`, `orders` is split
   into 64 separate `FlatIntTable`s (`8,192` 16-byte slots × 64 = **`8.0 MiB`**,
   `8×` larger than the 1 MiB per-core L2!). Every worker scatters 18.97M probes
   randomly across all 64 tables, collapsing IPC to **`1.46`** (`23.99M` L2d
   refills).
3. **Quiet ablation proof:** Adding `probe_mark_radix_flat_int` (`no_pushdown +
fast_semi`) alone cuts `morsel_no_pushdown` by **`−480.8 ms` CPU (`2,206.9 →
1,726.0 ms`)** and **`−59.9 ms` wall (`297.4 → 237.5 ms`)**, leaving the
   remaining `~448 ms` CPU gap vs. `stock_default` due strictly to the `8.0 MiB`
   L2 miss footprint.

#### Why `morsel_pushdown` (`226.5 ms`) Still Trails `stock_default` (`201.1 ms`) by `+25.4 ms`

`perf record` (`44K` samples, `0` lost) pinpoints the exact `+139 ms` CPU /
`+366M` cycle gap:

1. **`JoinBloomFilterExpr::evaluate` is #1 at `26.43%` of ALL cycles (`~377 ms`
   CPU / `~47 ms` wall!):**
   - `SplitBlockBloomFilter` (`join_filter.rs:100–167`) allocates `16,384`
     unaligned `[u32; 8]` blocks (`512 KiB`, straddling 64-byte cache lines) and
     checks all 8 scalar words unconditionally inside
     `BooleanBuffer::collect_bool` on **all `30.0M` `lineitem` rows** (not the
     `18.97M` post-`l_receiptdate > l_commitdate` rows!).
   - Worse, at `3.8%` uniform random selectivity, Zstd must still decompress
     every 131K-row page of the 4-byte `l_commitdate` and `l_receiptdate`
     columns (`ZSTD + HUF + RLE = 26.7% ≈ 378 ms` CPU vs. `407.5 ms` full bulk
     decode in `morsel_no_pushdown`!). So `RowFilter` saves only `~30 ms` of
     decode while paying **`+377 ms` Bloom + `~60 ms` `RowSelection::and_then` /
     `filter_native` + `+26 ms` `AtomicSplitBlockBloomFilter::insert`
     (`__aarch64_ldset4_relax = 1.86%`, `join_finalize: 10.4` vs. `3.7 ms`)**.
2. **`keep_dynamic_preds_in_bulk = !self.has_join()` (`morsel.rs:4806`) traps
   `lineitem` in `RowFilter`:** When `AdaptiveRowFilter` probes 2 `BulkDecode`
   RGs on `lineitem`, `keep_dynamic_preds_in_bulk = false` drops the Bloom
   filter and feeds raw rows into the unspecialized `BuildSemi` loop
   (`bulk_ns/row = 68.0` vs. `rf_ns/row = 33.4`), locking `lineitem` into
   `RowFilter` (`273 RF / 31 Bulk`). The 24–31 `BulkDecode` RGs also explain why
   `probe_mark_all_matches` still consumes **`4.84%` (`~69 ms` CPU)** in
   `morsel_pushdown`.
3. **Quiet ablation & unmeasured fused-Bloom estimate:** In quiet 5-round
   ablation, `bloom_fast + fast_semi` recovers **`−69.8 ms` CPU (`−12.1 ms`
   wall: `209.3 → 197.2 ms` exec)** and `force_bulk + keep_dyn + bloom_fast +
fast_semi` recovers **`−15.4 ms` wall (`209.3 → 194.0 ms` exec, `par =
7.05×`)**. Even with NEON + 32-byte alignment + prefetch, probing a 512 KiB
   8-word Bloom filter on all `30M` rows inside Parquet `RowFilter` (building a
   `BooleanArray` and `RowSelection::and_then`) still executes `~300 ms` of
   Bloom and `RowSelection` work. **To close the remaining gap and beat

   to need a compact 1-word (64-bit) register-blocked Bloom prefilter fused
   directly into `probe_mark_radix_flat_int`**, running AFTER

### 3.3 TPC-H Q11: `135.2 ms` vs. `stock_default 112.9 ms` (`+22.3 ms` / `1.20×`)

#### Physical Plan Comparison

- **Both engines scan `partsupp ⋈ supplier ⋈ nation` twice (no `CrossJoin`):**
  Both Stock (`ScalarSubqueryExec`) and Morsel (`Stage scalar_sq_0`) evaluate
  the uncorrelated `HAVING` threshold (`sum(ps_supplycost * ps_availqty) *

- **Crucial Stock advantage — `ArrayMap` (`EXPLAIN ANALYZE`):** Stock builds an
  **`ArrayMap`** (`array_map_created_count = 1`, `781.2 KiB` for all 50,000
  `supplier` rows, `32 KiB` for 1 `nation` row) and probes all `8.0M` `partsupp`
  rows across both scans via direct `O(1)` integer indexing with **zero
  hashing** (`ArrayMap::get_matched_indices = 8.01%` CPU, **`2.05` IPC**!).
  Quiet `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` for `stock_default` shows `partsupp DataSourceExec =
448.5 ms` (`234.7 + 213.9 ms`) and `HashJoinExec = 109.8 ms` (`42.9 + 13.0 +
41.8 + 12.0 ms`; `exec = 99.9 ms`, `exec CPU = 662.4 ms`).
- **Morsel (`morsel_pushdown`):** Filters `supplier` via `nation`'s dynamic
  filter (`n_nationkey = 7` → `2,003` German suppliers in `SingleHashTable`,
  `R = 0`), then pushes a `ps_suppkey` Bloom filter (`4%` selectivity) into both
  32-RG `partsupp` scans (`64` RGs total). Yet `morsel_pushdown` still trails
  `stock_default` by **`+26.2 ms` exec (`126.1` vs. `99.9 ms`; `−3.8 ms` plan
  gives `+22.3 ms` total)** despite retiring **3.5% fewer instructions**
  (`3,036M` vs. `3,145M`) and **7.8% fewer L2d refills** (`9.11M` vs. `9.88M`)!

#### Exact Attribution of `q11`'s `+26.2 ms` Exec Deficit

1. **Adaptive probe tax on 32-RG `partsupp` scans (`−12.9 ms` wall / `−72.5 ms`
   CPU in quiet ablation):**
   - Quiet `[DIAG_ADAPT]` probes show `partsupp` runs **`1.02×–1.21×` faster in
     `BulkDecode` than `RowFilter`** (`92.7–96.4` vs. `104.5–112.3 ns/row`;
     `scalar_sq_0`: `112.3` vs. `92.7 ns/row` [`1.21×`], `main`: `104.5` vs.
     `96.4 ns/row` [`1.08×`], consistent with `1.00–1.31×` across repeated quiet
     runs).
   - Why does `RowFilter` lose even at `4%` selectivity? `ps_supplycost` is a
     `FIXED_LEN_BYTE_ARRAY` (`Decimal128`, `451.5 KiB` compressed / `757.5 KiB`
     uncompressed per RG, `PLAIN + RLE_DICTIONARY`). At `4%` uniform random
     selectivity (`160K / 4M`), `RowSelection` produces ~154K `Select(1) /
Skip(24)` segments that **cannot skip any Zstd pages** while adding
     `JoinBloomFilterExpr` (`6.02%` CPU = `47.5 ms`) and `RowSelection`
     overhead!
   - Because each `partsupp` scan has only **32 RGs across 8 workers (`4`
     RGs/worker)**, wave-1 (`2 RF + 2 Bulk + 4 default RF`) and wave-2 dispatch
     run **`25–34 / 64` `partsupp` RGs (`40–53%` of the entire table!) in slow
     `RowFilter`** before the controller commits to `BulkDecode` (`RF/Bulk =
38/30`).
   - Once committed to `BulkDecode`, `keep_dynamic_preds_in_bulk = false`
     (`morsel.rs:4806`) drops the tiny `4 KiB` L1-resident Bloom filter, sending
     all remaining rows into `probe_collect_matches` (`9.63%` CPU = `~76 ms`).
   - **Quiet ablation proof:** `force_bulk + keep_dyn + bloom_fast` cuts `q11`
     by **`−72.5 ms` CPU (`791.0 → 718.5 ms`)** and **`−12.9 ms` exec wall
     (`123.7 → 110.8 ms`)** (`force_bulk` alone saves `−4.9 ms`, whereas
     `force_rf` regresses `+11.2 ms` wall / `+25.5 ms` CPU!).
2. **4 sequential 1-RG tiny builds (`~6–8 ms` wall / `27.4 ms` `ready_wait`):**
   `scalar_sq_0_build_1` (`nation`, `1.1 ms`), `scalar_sq_0_build_0` (`supplier`,
   `3.3 ms`), `build_1` (`nation`, `0.9 ms`), and `build_0` (`supplier`,
   `2.1 ms`) each spawn 8 Tokio workers, wake 1 worker for a single RG, and park
   7 workers (`ready_wait = 6.4 + 8.5 + 5.8 + 6.7 = 27.4 ms`), lowering
   parallelism (`6.25×` vs. `6.63×`). Moreover, `nation` (`1` row) and
   `supplier` (`2,003` rows) are built identically twice.
3. **`SingleHashTable` hashing vs. Stock's `ArrayMap` (`~4–5 ms` wall):**
   `probe_collect_matches` (`9.63%` CPU, `~76 ms`) hashes `ps_suppkey` against
   the 2,003-row `SingleHashTable`, whereas a direct `50,000`-entry bitset
   (`6.25 KiB`, 100% L1d-resident) or dense `ArrayMap` probes `ps_suppkey` in 2
   instructions with zero hashing (and also eliminates the catastrophic `2.21×`
   L2d refill / `1.21` IPC cliff in `morsel_no_pushdown`).
4. **Bonus (beyond Stock parity — beats DuckDB `95.7 ms`):** Notice

### Concrete Implementation Notes for Follow-Up PRs

1. **Rank 1 (`dag.rs:660`):** Batches waiting inside `ensure_stage`'s
   `mpsc::channel` are not charged to `MemoryPool` until the receiver calls
   `MaterializedStage::try_push`. Simply raising `sink_capacity.max(4)` to `64`
   would allow up to `16×` more uncharged batches in flight and exceed
   `MAX_RESULT_CAPACITY = 32` (`morsel.rs:118`), risking OOM under tight memory
   limits. Quiet capacity sweeps (`4 / 16 / 32 / 64` → `161.5 / 138.7 / 141.8 /
138.9 ms`) confirm that **`result_capacity(workers)` (`= 16` at 8 workers,
   already capped at `MAX_RESULT_CAPACITY = 32`) captures the full `−22.8 ms`
   win**. To capture this gain safely across all multi-stage radix aggregates
   (`q13`, `q16`, `q18`), either (a) raise `ensure_stage`'s channel capacity to
   `result_capacity(stage_pipeline.workers)` (`≤ MAX_RESULT_CAPACITY = 32`) or
   (b) charge `SharedStageMemory::try_add_batch` **before** enqueueing (or
   collect per-partition batches directly into a pool-charged
   `Mutex<MaterializedStage>`, removing the intermediate channel altogether).
2. **Rank 2 (`hash_join.rs` + `join_filter.rs`):**
   - Port the `probe_collect_radix_flat_int` batch-inlined macro pattern to
     `BuildSemi`/`BuildAnti` (`probe_mark_radix_flat_int`): single `mix_u64`,
     zero per-row enum dispatch.
   - Align `SplitBlockBloomFilter` blocks (`#[repr(C, align(32))]`), add a
     fast 1-word (64-bit, 2-bit-per-key or 4-hash) register-blocked prefilter
     option (`64–256 KiB`, strictly L2-resident!) directly on
     `FinalizedHashTable`, and evaluate it inside `probe_mark_radix_flat_int` /
     `probe_collect_radix_flat_int` so `BulkDecode` pipelines reject `95%+` of
     non-matching probe keys in L2 cache without touching the `4–8 MiB`
     64-partition radix tables **or** allocating `BooleanArray`/`RowSelection`.
3. **Rank 3 (`morsel.rs:2454`):** Group `run_build_pipelines` into dependency
   waves based on `dynamic_join_filters` edges; within any wave of independent
   builds (like `q16`'s `part` and `supplier`), launch them concurrently under a
   shared `JoinSet` so a 1-RG build never stalls 7 idle cores.
4. **Rank 4 (`buffered.rs` + `morsel.rs:4806`):**
   - Refine `can_skip_columns` and the `estimated_scan_rgs < 12` heuristic: when
     a scan has no dynamic join filter and projected payload columns represent
     `< 25%` of total decoded column width (like `q16` `part` where `p_partkey`
     is 1 of 4 columns, or `q22` `customer` where `c_phone` + `c_acctbal` are
     both in the predicate), default to `BulkDecode` instead of `RowFilter`.
   - For medium scans (`12 <= estimated_scan_rgs <= 48`, like `q11` `partsupp`
     32 RGs), reduce wave-1 default dispatch while probing (`probe_rgs_per_mode
= 1` and alternate `RF`/`Bulk` during wave 1 instead of defaulting all
     remaining wave-1 RGs to `RowFilter`), and pass `keep_dynamic_preds_in_bulk
= true` when the build table is selective (or rely on Rank 2's in-join
     register-blocked Bloom).
5. **Rank 5 (`hash_join.rs`):** Add a dense `ArrayMap` / bitset representation
   for `SingleHashTable` when single-column integer build keys have small span
   (`max - min <= 262_144` and density `>= 1%`, covering `q11` `supplier` both
   filtered `[1..50,000]` and unfiltered), matching Stock's `2.05` IPC lookup.
